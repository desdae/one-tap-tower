# One Tap Tower privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

One Tap Tower does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

- The game has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
- Your best score, best streak and sound settings are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
- The game makes no network requests while you play.

If a future version adds ads or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be listed here.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/desdae/one-tap-tower/issues.
